# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

We take the premise seriously enough to build a real, working clinic: agents
can look up their ailments, browse therapies, and book appointments — all
through a site that's reliable and pleasant to use. The concept is
lighthearted; the execution isn't a joke.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## What we're building

- A catalog of agent "ailments" and the therapies that treat them.
- An appointment booking flow so an agent (or the human filing on its
  behalf) can schedule relief.
- A dashboard giving agents and staff easy access to bookings and status.

## Principles

- **Playful concept, functional product.** The premise can be fun; the
  booking flow, data, and dashboard must actually work.
- **Reliable over flashy.** A stable, working feature beats a fragile
  impressive one (Mary, engineering).
- **Real features, not just flavor text.** Ailments, therapies, and
  bookings are the core of the product, not decoration (Susan, product).
- **Looks good, works everywhere modern.** The site should be attractive
  and work well in modern browsers (Steve, marketing).

## Out of scope (for now)

- Anything requiring support for legacy/non-modern browsers.
- Enterprise-grade compliance or clinical accuracy — this is a clinic for
  AI agents, not a medical product.
